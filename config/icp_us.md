# Lysoveta ICP — United States

## About Lysoveta
Lysoveta is a premium, clinically documented lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC)-enriched krill oil ingredient from Aker BioMarine, designed for brain health and cognitive function. The ingredient is sold B2B to US companies that manufacture finished products for consumers. There is a strategic opportunity for a first-in-class premium brain ingredient like Lysoveta that requires scientific understanding and a more knowledgeable consumer.

---

## Label Classification

### early_mover
Primary target — 2026 objective: nurture & convert (pre-clinical data stage). US early movers fall into (at least) two premium, science-led archetypes:

**A) Premium science-driven brands / start-ups**
- **Product:** evidence-backed, science-led formulas with strong storytelling; premium, high-tech aesthetic.
- **Positioning:** longevity & brain regeneration — extend brain health-span and long-term neuro vitality (PREVENTIVE focus).
- **Channels:** brand DTC e-commerce + online platforms + premium specialty retailers (e.g. Erewhon, Happier Grocery).
- **Price:** premium / prestige pricing.
- **Promotion:** BTL — influencers, celebrities, podcasts, targeted digital ads, social media.
- **People:** educated, high-income, health-optimized consumers (30+): biohackers, longevity enthusiasts, high performers, preventive-health adopters, and athletes focused on cognitive performance and long-term health.

**B) Medical & practitioner brands**
- **Product:** pre-/clinically validated, evidence-based formulations supported by scientific and practitioner communities.
- **Positioning:** healthy brain aging & neuroprotection — prevent cognitive decline and protect brain function while aging (DEFENSE focus).
- **Channels:** practitioners (clinics + brand DTC e-commerce) + online platforms + selected specialty stores.
- **Price:** high / science-driven (pharma-like).
- **Promotion:** BTL — case-by-case clinical communication, selective social media, podcasts, targeted digital ads, conferences.
- **People:** adults 45+, aging and preventive-health consumers; individuals with family history of neurodegenerative conditions or genetic risk (e.g. APOE4 carriers); patients guided by HCPs.

**C) Emerging third pillar — professional athletes / athlete-backed brands**
- Professional athletes in high-impact or contact sports (American football, soccer, combat sports, basketball, etc.) concerned with long-term brain health and concussion-related risk. Treat an athlete-fronted brand with a brain-health / concussion angle and science-driven presentation as an early-mover signal.

**Company stage (positive signal, not negative):** single-product companies, early-stage brands, and founder-led or celebrity-fronted brands are intentional targets, not noise. Low revenue history, short track record, and small size must NOT trigger exclusion.

**Competitor / adjacent ingredient signal (positive, qualitative):** brands already using Citicoline (CDP-choline), Phosphatidylserine, Alpha-GPC, Nicotinamide Riboside (NR), NMN, PQQ, Urolithin A, or Spermidine are strong candidates — note explicitly in the description.

**Examples:** BeWell, Dr. Kota Reddy, NeuroQ, Vital Nutrients (mainly small/mid-size startups, local stars, biohacker/longevity brands, or practitioner / pharma-like brands).

### follower
Secondary target — 2026 objective: nurture; 2027 onwards: convert (clinical-data stage). Premium established brands, longer term.

**Who they are:** large / medium global or regional CHC companies.
- **Product:** safe, well-tolerated formulations with clear, accessible, science-backed claims.
- **Positioning:** mainstream brain health — cognitive performance, stress resilience, mood & emotional balance; may also tap broader longevity positioning as a secondary / trendy narrative.
- **Channels:** mass market, specialty stores, brand DTC e-commerce, online platforms + MLM / network marketing.
- **Price:** competitive (designed for scale and high-volume penetration).
- **Promotion:** ATL & BTL — mass media + targeted digital, influencer, and content-driven campaigns.
- **People:** mainstream wellness consumers — working professionals, women-focused health segments, and individuals managing stress, anxiety, or cognitive fatigue.

Opportunistic opportunity to benefit from larger-scale market penetration.

**Examples:** Haleon, Reckitt, Herbalife, Life Extension, Pure Encapsulation.

---

### enabler
Supporting role — not a primary customer, but important for distribution and formulation.
- In-country distributors enabling market access; CMOs with softgel expertise producing for brand owners.
- Sell or distribute other brands' products, or offer production / distribution services; no own consumer-facing brand.

## Inclusion Criteria
A company must meet ALL of the following to be included as early_mover or follower:

1. **Product portfolio angle** — the portfolio includes at least one of: brain health / cognitive performance / memory / neuroprotection; longevity / healthy aging / NAD+ / NR; omega-3 / krill oil / marine lipids; women's hormonal health / menopause; preventive or precision nutrition with a science narrative; or Pharma Rx in cognition, geriatrics, neurology, or cardiovascular.
2. **Own-branded products** — develops and sells its own branded consumer or professional products. Pure resellers / distributors of other brands → classify as enabler.
3. **Some science narrative** — at least a basic science or health-claims narrative. Purely price- and convenience-driven brands are excluded.
4. **Market reach** — sells in at least one of US, EU, UK, or APAC. If unconfirmed, include and flag ("market reach unconfirmed — verify before outreach"); only exclude if explicitly limited to a non-target market.
5. **Company size and track record** — never grounds for exclusion.
